keycloak-authz-lab

Authorization as executable code, not as a wiki page.

Role-based authorization with Spring Cloud Gateway, Keycloak and a Spring Boot resource server. The business domain is deliberately trivial — one invoice resource with fields of different sensitivity. The point is that four roles see four different projections of the same resource, and every cell of that matrix is covered by a test. Two strictly separated test levels: a slice suite with simulated JWTs that runs on plain mvn test with no Docker, and an integration suite that boots a real Keycloak with Testcontainers behind its own Maven profile.

dian-ubl

Colombian electronic invoicing, with no floating point anywhere near money.

A Rust library and CLI that builds and validates electronic invoices in the UBL 2.1 profile DIAN requires: a typed domain model, exact integer-scaled monetary arithmetic, the DIAN NIT check-digit algorithm, CUFE computation over SHA-384, and deterministic UBL serialization. The validator reports every problem it finds, each anchored to a field path, instead of stopping at the first — because an invoice rejected four times for four different reasons is four round trips with the tax authority. v1 is implemented, with input fixtures and normative golden files; XAdES signing and web-service submission are explicitly out of scope.

latam-tech-jobs

A free daily dataset of LATAM-eligible tech roles, normalized across six ATS.

Roughly 1,350–1,500 postings from about 45 job boards, refreshed every day at 07:00 UTC and normalized into one schema across six different applicant tracking systems. No token, no signup, no login — two URLs. It publishes per-source health metadata alongside the data, so a broken upstream is visible rather than silently returning fewer rows. That reporting is the part I care about: a pipeline that fails quietly is worse than one that fails.
